About the salon

GYELMO LUXE Salon began with one chair and a simple idea: beauty care should feel like visiting a friend's home. Today we are a team of six, serving families, students, office workers and brides from across Thimphu and nearby valleys.

Our stylists trained in Bhutan and abroad, and they still learn from the herbal knowledge passed down in their own families.

We keep appointments unhurried, and we never push products you do not need.

Local ingredients

Herbal masks, oils and rinses made with plants from the region.

Clean and careful

Every tool is sterilised, and every guest gets fresh towels and capes.

Honest advice

We tell you what will work for your hair and skin, and what will not.

Common questions

Answers to what guests ask most.

Do I need to book in advance?

We welcome walk-ins when there is space, but booking is best, especially on weekends and before festivals.

How long does a visit take?

A haircut takes about 45 minutes. Facials take about an hour. Colour and bridal services take longer.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Yes. Please call us at least one day before so we can offer the time to another guest.

Do you offer bridal services at home?

Yes, within Thimphu. Travel costs depend on the location and time of day.

Are your products safe for sensitive skin?

We use gentle products and can do a small patch test first. Please tell us about any allergies before your treatment.

How can I pay?

We accept cash and mobile banking transfers.
